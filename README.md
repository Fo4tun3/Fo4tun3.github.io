## Welcome

Welcome to my professional portfolio repository. I am a **First Class
Cyber Security graduate** of the Air Force Institute of Technology
(AFIT), Kaduna, Nigeria, with a CGPA of **4.89/5.00**. I currently
contribute to teaching, student mentoring, and research activities as a
Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant.

My work sits at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, machine
learning, and cybersecurity**, with a focus on developing practical
approaches to threat detection, secure intelligent systems, and
privacy-aware AI.

## Research focus

My research interests include:

-   **AI for Cybersecurity:** intelligent threat detection, phishing
    detection, zero-day attack detection, and security analytics.
-   **Trustworthy and Privacy-Preserving AI:** explainability,
    privacy-aware systems, federated learning, and responsible AI.
-   **IoT, Edge AI & Intelligent Systems:** applying machine learning to
    security challenges in connected and resource-constrained
    environments.
-   **NLP & Large Language Models:** language-based security analysis,
    generative AI, and LLM-assisted cybersecurity workflows.

## Selected areas of work

The portfolio highlights research and projects involving:

-   Federated learning and edge AI for IoT threat detection.
-   Machine-learning approaches to zero-day vulnerability and attack
    detection.
-   Entropy-based analysis of phishing and social-engineering messages.
-   Privacy-preserving AI and data protection.
-   LLM-assisted security testing and intelligent cybersecurity
    workflows.
-   AI governance, transparency, and accountability.

For publication status and complete details, please refer to the
Publications section of the portfolio and the linked publisher, journal,
or conference pages where available.
